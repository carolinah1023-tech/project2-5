About Sweet Caro's Bakery

Sweet Caro's Bakery started when I became a stay at home mom. I wanted something fun to do while I was home with my kids and make sure what they consume was good for them so I started baking. At first, I baked for my family because I enjoyed it.

Before I knew it, my friends and family started asking me to bake for birthdays and special occasions. That gave me the idea to start sharing my baked goods online and taking small orders.

I love using fresh ingredients and taking my time to make everything from scratch. My dream is to one day have my own little bakery where people can enjoy homemade treats that are made with love.

Thank you for stopping by Sweet Caro's Bakery. I hope you enjoy my homemade treats as much as my family does!
